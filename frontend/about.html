ABOUT THE PROJECT

ClaimCheck AI

Multimodal Claim Verification using Artificial Intelligence.

The Problem

False and misleading information can spread rapidly through social media, websites and messaging platforms. Traditional fact-checking requires users to manually search multiple sources, compare information and determine whether a claim is reliable.

🔎

Our Solution

ClaimCheck AI is designed to allow users to submit text, images or text combined with images. The system identifies factual claims, searches reliable sources, compares supporting and contradicting evidence and produces an understandable verdict.

🤖

Important Note

AI-generated verification should be treated as an evidence-assistance tool. Users should review the cited sources before making important decisions.
